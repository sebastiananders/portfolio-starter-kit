# Started juggling again

If I have two meetings back-to-back, there’s basically no meaningful work happening in between. I’ve stopped trying to do deep work in those short gaps. I need time to process the previous meeting and prepare for the next one. There’s simply no mental space left for serious deep work.

So I started juggling again. It actually has three benefits: it helps me think more clearly, it keeps me off social media, and I’m not just sitting around. All good things. By the time the next meeting starts, I feel energized and I’ve already had space to think.
