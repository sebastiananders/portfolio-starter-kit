On a business trip. It is dark already and the internet on my train is slow as fuck. So I use the time to reflect a little what had happened in the last weeks. Spoiler: they were crazy.

Starting with my significant other. She is the most important person in my life. She has found a new job. In my opinion she is the best sales person on the planet. The cool companies CEOs should have a cage fight to determine who gets to work with her. But in this case nobody asks me so she had to go through the painstaking interview process. She did great. I am proud and we’ll party, once I return.

I also can party. My fire fighter training ended with an exam day. I am now officially a certified fire fighter. Proud to protect and serve my community with such a honorable purpose. I learned so many things during the training. How to assess dangerous situation and make the right decisions to help people in emergency situations. Happy to serve!

Many more great things happened. To much for one blog post - at least it feels like it. Lenin once said: “There are decades where nothing happens; and there are weeks where decades happen”. 

I am just incredibly thankful to be able to move forward. Nothing holds me back but me.
