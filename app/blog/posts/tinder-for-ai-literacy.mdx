## Building Tinder for AI literacy. 

My partner showed me a video about a cat doing stuff. It does not matter what it really was, in detail. She was so much into it. Laughing and cheering. Spoiler: it was AI. After I told her, she got upset. Like a lot. I do not want people to be so uncertain about AI content. It abvisouly is a big difference to people if something really happended or not. It is about self care and protection. 

Core Interaction: Swipe → Learn

Think of this as Tinder meets Duolingo for AI literacy.

Step 1 — Cold judgment (no spoilers)

User sees:
	•	Image or video
	•	Simple prompt: “AI or Not AI?”
	•	Two actions:
	•	Swipe right → AI
	•	Swipe left → Not AI
	•	(Optional: Swipe up = Unsure)

No metadata. No comments. No hints.

This preserves the gut instinct moment.

⸻

Step 2 — Reveal & reflection

Immediately after swipe, you show:

1. Outcome
Not “correct/incorrect” in a shaming way, but:
	•	Community lean: AI / Not AI / Mixed
	•	Confidence level: Low → High
	•	A short line:
“This one is controversial. People are split.”
or
“Strong consensus: likely AI-generated.”

2. Your sentiment vs others
A small vizualisation
