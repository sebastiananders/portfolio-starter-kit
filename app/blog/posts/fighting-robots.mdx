Also I don’t care if they’re dancing. I couldn’t care less. What I need is robots to do hard manual labour. To repair the streets. Filling potholes. Support fire departments, making dangerous jobs obsolete. I’d like to read news about the robot revolution and how we haven’t had a single accident on the manufacturing shop floor since then. People only need to work max 4h a day and get payed fully. They are being assisted by machines and can return home safely and rested. They have plenty of time and energy for the family. 

Military? Great! You don’t have to waste the life of soldiers anymore. But what is war worth, if two rival head of states throwing metal at each other? War will be automated. We only get the news that borders have shifted, while we drink our coffee.
