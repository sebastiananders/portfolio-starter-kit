It is February already. This post gives you some really cool resources focusing on design in the AI era. There are fundamentals of course. But many things also change, when it comes to HCI. Because the C is more likely to become an entity itself. It is not CRUD anymore. It is now doing stuff on its own.

Here are some nice helper sites to get a) a nice overview about HCI design for AI and b) more detailed style and interaction pattern to apply in you own workflow.

### Shape of AI

https://www.shapeof.ai
The rise of AI has caused a paradigm shift in how people interact with technology. Our interfaces may evolve, but the foundations of great design are more relevant than ever. This is the UX of AI.

### The AI Interaction Atlas

(link)[https://ai-interaction.com]
A shared language for designing human-AI interactions.

### Make Claude Code build Figma components

https://figma-guide-toec.vercel.app
How to make Claude Code build Figma button components for you, live on your canvas. No coding experience needed. I literally have none.

All three are quite cool to actually learn a little here and there. Because nothing is more important than reacting fast to changes in your very discipline.
